CATALA is an outdoor lifestyle brand born on the Pacific Northwest coast.

Built around the things we'd rather be doing — surfing, exploring, travelling, getting outside and chasing good days.

It's for surfers, outdoorsmen and women, explorers, and anyone else who'd rather be out there than sitting still.

You don't have to surf. You don't have to live on the coast. You just have to get it.
